Common questions

How many sprays should I use?

Two to three sprays are enough for daytime. For strong scents, start with one or two on the neck and wrists.

Where should I spray?

Spray on pulse points: wrists, neck and behind the ears. Warm skin helps the scent open up. Dab, don’t rub.

Why does a perfume smell different on me?

Skin type, diet and body heat change how the notes develop. Wait 30 minutes after spraying before you decide.

How do I store my bottle?

Keep it in a cool, dark place, away from sunlight and heat. Avoid leaving it in the car.
